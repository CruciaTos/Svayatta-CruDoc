# CBCT 3D viewer: roadmap (not built yet)

The 3D viewer opens inside CruDoc (Axial, 3D, Coronal, Sagittal with linked
crosshairs). These are planned next and were listed on the old placeholder screen:

- **Panoramic from CBCT**: draw or auto-detect the arch; numbered cross-sections along it
- **Nerve canal**: trace the mandibular canal; distance to canal on every measurement
- **Measurements in 3D**: distances, angles and lesion volume, saved to the study
- **Airway**: volume and the narrowest cross-section
- **Sinus and TMJ views**: both condyles side by side
- **Implant planning**: implant library, bone density along the implant, canal warning
- **Export**: STL for 3D printing and a rotating GIF

Also still to do from `handoff/cbct_3d_viewer_build_guide.md`: preview-then-full
loading, poster images for an instant first frame, saved view state, the full
right-hand panel (histogram, slab, threshold, opacity), orientation letters,
scale bars, and snapshots as key images.
